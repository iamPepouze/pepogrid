# New games rotate across technique families

For every difficulty except Easy, a new game comes from the technique family played least recently; a family with no unplayed puzzles is skipped until the cycle completes. Hard's bank also caps XY-Wing puzzles at 50%. Without this, random bank puzzles were about 95% XY-Wing at Hard, so consecutive games hinged on the same trick. Easy is all hidden singles, so it has nothing to rotate.

## Consequences

Small families come round as often as large ones: Hard's three Claiming puzzles take one game in four while they last.
