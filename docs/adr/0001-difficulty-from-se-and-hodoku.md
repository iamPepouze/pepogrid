# Difficulty comes from two ratings: SE picks, HoDoKu confirms

A puzzle's difficulty is chosen by its SE rating (Easy 1.0–1.5, Medium 1.6–2.6, Hard 2.7–4.2, Expert 4.3–6.5, Master 6.6–8.0), and its HoDoKu level must match (Easy, Medium, Hard, Unfair, Extreme respectively), otherwise the puzzle is rejected. SE alone ignores how long a solve is and HoDoKu alone weighs techniques differently; requiring both to agree keeps each difficulty consistent in both hardest step and overall effort. Puzzles above SE 8.0 are excluded as trial-and-error territory.

## Consequences

Agreement is rare at the top: only about 0.4% of SE 4.3–6.5 candidates are rated Unfair by HoDoKu, which makes the Expert bank slow to build.
