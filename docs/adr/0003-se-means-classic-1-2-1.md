# "SE" means classic Sudoku Explainer 1.2.1

SE ratings are produced by SukakuExplainer run with its technique set restricted to the classic Sudoku Explainer 1.2.1 techniques, not by the fork's defaults. The fork adds techniques such as the WXYZ-Wing family and strong-link fish that shift ratings away from the published community scale, and the difficulty ranges in ADR-0001 are defined on that classic scale.
